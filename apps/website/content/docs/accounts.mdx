This page is a placeholder; its guide is still to be written.

- **Mavio accounts** sign in to every server you reach with one account on mavio.dev.
- **Standalone servers** keep their own users and need no mavio.dev.
- **Homes** share devices among your family, each person with a profile of their own.
- **Mavio Pass** pays for the services mavio.dev runs, such as the relay.
