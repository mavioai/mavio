Mavio is a media server you run yourself. It organizes your movies, shows, music, books and photos, and plays them on your TV, phone and computer, at home or away.

## The parts

- **The server** runs on your computer or NAS and holds your libraries.
- **The apps** play from your servers on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Android, Android TV, Windows and Linux, and the web UI in any browser.
- **mavio.dev** is the optional account service: one sign-in for every server you reach, secure names and certificates for your servers, and the relay.
